In the office, Claude waits at its desk while its subagents work, so they no longer report to an
empty chair.

## Fixed

- **Subagents reported to an empty desk.** When Claude's turn ended while subagents still worked
  in the background (or a workflow's agents did), Claude went off to the lounge for a coffee.
  The subagents then came to report at Claude's empty desk. Claude now stays at its desk until
  the last of them has reported and left, and goes to the lounge after that. While it waits, the
  people list says *Waiting* (*Waiting for its subagents*) and the activity log says *Finished its
  turn, waiting for its subagents*.
- **Subagents stayed after an account switch.** When baton went on with another account, it
  stopped the Claude Code the background subagents ran in, yet they stayed in the office, at
  work, for up to 15 minutes. They now go home at the shift change.

  Claude의 차례가 끝나면, Claude가 라운지로 커피를 마시러 갔습니다. 그러면 서브에이전트가 Claude의 빈 책상에
  보고하러 왔습니다. 이제 Claude는 마지막 서브에이전트가 보고를 마치고 퇴근할 때까지 책상에 남아 있다가, 그 뒤에
  라운지로 갑니다. 기다리는 동안 사무실 사람들 목록에는 *기다리는 중*(*서브에이전트를 기다리는 중*)이, 활동
  로그에는 *차례를 마치고 서브에이전트를 기다리는 중*이 나옵니다.

  함께 퇴근합니다.
